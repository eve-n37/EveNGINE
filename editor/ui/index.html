THINGS WORTH FINDING

Inventory items

Define rewards and keys, then assign them to objects in the inspector.

Pickups and chests award items. Doors can require one and optionally consume it. The runtime bag opens with Q or I; A/D change pages. Item and interaction progress is saved with F5 and restored with F9.

A REASON TO EXPLORE

Quests

Build objectives, define rewards, and offer quests through dialogue choices.

Item objectives consume their requested items when turned in. Talking and visiting count after acceptance; already activated objects count immediately. Rewards are awarded once. Press J in-game to open the journal.

LET PLAYERS ANSWER

Dialogue

Connect conversation nodes and add choices, conditions, and quest actions.

Up to three choices per node. W/S selects; E confirms; Space closes. Conditions hide choices that do not apply. Every node must connect to the starting node.
